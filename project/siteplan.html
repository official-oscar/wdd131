Benin City Street Food Guide - Site Plan

1. Site Name

Benin City Street Food Guide

This name clearly tells visitors what the site is about and targets both locals and tourists looking for authentic food experiences in Edo State. The name is memorable and uses keywords people would search for.

Optional domain: beninfoodguide.com

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase the most popular and affordable street foods in Benin City. It will provide descriptions, average prices, and locations to help residents and visitors discover new food spots. The site will also allow users to submit their own food recommendations through a form.

3. Scenarios

Questions from our target audience:

  • "Where can I find the best and cheapest Boli and Fish in Benin City tonight?"
  • "What are the top 5 must-try street foods for a first-time visitor to Edo State?"

4. Color Schema

Chosen to feel warm, appetizing, and reflect Nigerian markets.
